Zero-Upload Local Sandbox Architecture

Why Local Browser Processing Protects Your Privacy

Traditional online PDF converters upload your tax returns, legal contracts, and personal IDs to remote cloud servers. FileVault executes PDF assembly, splitting, rendering, and image compression locally inside your device's RAM using WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas APIs.

No Server Uploads by Default

Files are read via browser ArrayBuffer and Blob streams. Nothing leaves your computer or phone unless you explicitly configure an external AI endpoint.

Automatic Memory Cleanup

Temporary URL.createObjectURL handles and canvas buffers are explicitly revoked when you reset a tool or close the tab.

Browser Performance & Memory Guidelines

  • 01. Desktop & Laptop Browsers: Comfortably process PDFs up to 150 MB and 500+ pages depending on available system RAM.
  • 02. Android & Mobile Safari: Recommended up to 50 MB per batch to prevent mobile browser tab memory throttling.
  • 03. Encrypted PDFs: Password-locked PDFs are detected immediately so you know why a file cannot be modified without unlocking first.
  • 04. Optional AI & Future Pro Tier Ready: Built-in extractive summarization runs 100% locally; optional serverless proxy hooks are documented for teams connecting enterprise LLMs.
Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF documents ever uploaded to a remote server? ▼

No. All core PDF and image tools (Merge, Split, Image to PDF, Page Organizer, Compress Image, Extract Text, PDF to Images, Rotate, Page Numbering, Metadata, WebP converter, Scanner, and Tesseract OCR) execute directly inside your web browser. Because there is no server upload, we never make false claims about "deleting files from our servers after 1 hour"—your files never touch a server in the first place.

Why did Image Compressor tell me my image size did not decrease? ▼

FileVault never fabricates compression metrics. If you upload an already heavily-optimized JPEG or convert a simple graphic to a lossless PNG at 100% quality, the re-encoded canvas buffer may be equal to or larger than the original file. When that happens, we transparently alert you, display the exact byte comparison, and recommend lowering the quality slider or switching output format to WebP or JPEG.

What is the difference between "Extract PDF Text" and "OCR Scanned PDF"? ▼

Digital PDFs created from Word, Google Docs, or invoices store selectable vector text streams that our Extract PDF Text tool reads instantaneously using PDF.js. By contrast, scanned paper documents contain flat photographs of pages with zero embedded text characters. When our extractor detects a scanned page without selectable text, it notifies you and offers a 1-click transfer to our OCR Tool powered by Tesseract.js.

Does FileVault work on Android phones, tablets, and laptops? ▼

Yes. Every tool is built mobile-first with large touch targets, keyboard-accessible reorder buttons alongside drag-and-drop, and responsive grids that prevent horizontal overflow on small phone screens.
